Meet the Team at Healthy Valley Chiropractic

Get to Know Us

Dr. Jay Villella

Dr. Craig Baniszewski

Melissa, Office Manager & Case Manager

Melissa Schreck has been a dedicated part of the team at Healthy Valley Chiropractic for 17 years. She has a strong passion for helping people achieve better health and maintain long-term wellness. Over the years, Melissa has done just about everything in the office—of course, except adjusting (LOL). Her extensive experience and commitment to patient care make her an invaluable part of the practice.

Melissa is married to her best friend, Bobby, and together they are proud parents to their daughter, Kyndal, who is living her dream in the Orlando area while working at Disney.

Donovan, CA/Technician

Donovan is part of the Healthy Valley Chiropractic team, where he enjoys working with patients and supporting their journey toward better health. As a student in Health and Rehabilitation Sciences at The Pennsylvania State University, Donovan is building a strong foundation in rehabilitation and wellness. He values creating a welcoming environment and is committed to helping individuals feel supported, informed, and motivated as they work toward their wellness goals.

Cole, CA/Technician

I am a business management major that loves sports. I played baseball for over 16 years and even played in college. I also love to give baseball lessons to the youth on my time off and I just love to give back to my community. I am very dedicated to making everyone I can better and healthier. My favorite thing in the office is seeing my patients get so much better and becoming pain free.

Mitch, Case Manager

Mitch first came to the practice as a patient and later returned to join the team after eight years in healthcare and medical device sales. He’s passionate about building strong relationships and helping improve the lives of the patients he serves.

Erin, Front/Back Office

Erin may be the newest member of Healthy Valley Chiropractic, but she is no stranger to chiropractic care. A patient for over 35 years, Erin has also worked as a chiropractic assistant for more than 25 years. Her passion for wellness runs deep – her children even received their first adjustments before coming home.
Pennsylvania-grown, Erin recently returned from Colorado to be closer to her family and grandchildren. When she’s not enjoying the outdoors, she can be found in her mini jungle reading or writing, cooking, making jewelry, or sewing. Erin is deeply dedicated to health and wellness and feels honored to walk alongside patients on their individual wellness journeys.
