Healthy Valley Chiropractic Provides Solutions for Your Pain

State College Chiropractors Dr. Jay Villella and Dr. Craig Baniszewski have provided skilled health care for the community and since 1998. With a focus on correcting problems for the long-term, instead of treating symptoms, our team cares about helping you achieve your health goals. Our practice is concerned with more than just having you come in for an adjustment; we're a whole lifestyle wellness program.

Award Winning Care

Dr. Jay believes in continuing his education and has received certifications in spinal decompression therapy and neuropathy care. His skill and attention to patient care has also earned him recognition as the best in providing these services to his community.

Our Approach

In addition to what is done in the office, we give our patients instructions for things to do at home to support their care. We teach them about nutrition, exercises, and therapies they can do outside our office, providing a pathway to the best possible solution. As they reach one goal, they progress to the next level, until they reach their ultimate goal.

Our patients want to live happier, healthier lifestyles and understand there's no quick fix; things don't go away on their own. They want the best possible quality of life, and are willing to do the work to get there. About 40% of our patients have been with the practice for over 20 years, and it's really gratifying to see them maintain their health all these years.

How We Help

Some conditions we help include:

  • Disc issues
  • Neuropathy
  • Back pain
  • Neck pain
  • Headaches
  • Sciatica
  • Extremity pain
  • Postural/balance issues
  • Sports injuries
  • Knee pain
  • Hip pain
  • Shoulder pain
  • Ankle sprain
  • Plantar fasciitis, and more

With advanced technology like motion X-rays and thermographic scans, our team will find the root cause of your problem, correct it, and instruct you on how to maintain your health.
