Understanding the Importance of Knee Health

Knee pain can be quite debilitating, as it’s one of the most used and complex joints of the human body. Knee stability depends on four ligaments, muscles, and the joint structure. During a typical day, the knee goes through a full range of motions, from sitting, walking, twisting, etc.

The knees, along with the feet, also support the body’s weight and suffer from daily abuse as a result, causing damage to the muscles, cartilage, or the joint itself. While pain may be temporarily relieved with anti-inflammatory ointments or tablets, more severe cases need medical attention for more effective relief. Some patients have required surgical intervention to have their knee replaced.

Symptoms will vary from person to person, ranging from nagging to acute pain or discomfort while doing everyday chores. Some of the more common symptoms include:

  • Inflammation of the joint
  • Tenderness around the area
  • Instability (knee gives way for no reason)
  • Locking
  • A feeling of grinding
  • Popping
  • Stiffness
  • Injuries to the knee may also show up as body aches and back pain.
